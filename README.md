## Purpose

This repository documents my engagement with NASA's Transform to Open Science (TOPS) 101 curriculum — a five-module course covering the principles, tools, and practices of open science.

I am building this documentation in public for two reasons. First, as a direct application of what the course teaches: that knowledge grows faster when it is shared openly and structured for reuse. Second, as a foundation for an Open Science Club I am working to establish at Marist College, where I will be studying Chemistry and Data Science.

If you are a student, educator, or researcher new to open science, this repository is intended to be readable and useful to you — not just a personal notebook.

---

## What is NASA TOPS Open Science 101?

The NASA Transform to Open Science (TOPS) initiative developed this curriculum to accelerate the adoption of open science practices across research communities. The course covers five interconnected areas: the ethical foundations of open science, open data management, open-source software, open results and publishing, and reproducible analysis workflows.

More information: [https://nasa.github.io/Transform-to-Open-Science/](https://nasa.github.io/Transform-to-Open-Science/)
